About Our Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started with a simple idea: the neighborhood deserved a place to get honest, handmade bread. Our small team still bakes the way we did on day one, in small batches with simple ingredients and plenty of care.

Our Values

  • Handmade: Every loaf and pastry is shaped by hand.
  • Fresh: We bake every morning and never sell day-old bread as fresh.
  • Community: We donate unsold bread to local food pantries and support neighborhood events.
